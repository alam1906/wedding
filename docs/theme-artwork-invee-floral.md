# Invee Floral 1–6 — theme references and artwork

Created 2026-10-01. Six original transparent botanical illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool (generate mode), then encoded to 800 × 800 WebP with alpha preserved (`cwebp -q 82 -m 6 -sharp_yuv`). No third-party reference images are bundled. Fonts reuse the existing locally licensed font assets.

The reference layouts inform the six compositions; wedding names, photographs, quotes, dates, stories, gifts and RSVP continue to use the existing WeddingData contract. No reference-site placeholder copy, vendor/protocol sections, icons or arrows are added.

## Final generation prompts

### Floral Melati

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent ornamental botanical artwork for a wedding website, NOT a full web design.
Primary request: An airy asymmetrical wedding botanical spray with ivory white lilies, pale cream flower buds, long sage green leaves, olive foliage and soft champagne grasses, elegant translucent watercolor.
Composition/framing: one single complete L-shaped arrangement growing from the lower-left corner toward the top-left and bottom-right, with graceful stems sweeping diagonally. Entire arrangement visible, 6 percent clear margin around all leaves. Flowers cluster at the bottom-left, airy leaf tips above. Square canvas.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted watercolor illustration, delicate botanical detail, visible paper-like pigment within the artwork only.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background, isolated floral cutout with clean alpha edges.
Constraints: no background color, no paper rectangle, no border or frame, no lettering, no people, no wedding photo, no logo, no watermark. Original botanical composition.
```
